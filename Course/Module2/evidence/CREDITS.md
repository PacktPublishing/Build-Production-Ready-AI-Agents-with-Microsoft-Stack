# Evidence credits

The damage **photographs** in this folder are real, and openly licensed. Attribution below
is required by their licences and must travel with the files.

The repair-quote **documents** are synthetic, generated for the book's exercises. They
are marked SPECIMEN. Contoso Insurance, the suppliers and the claim references are fictional.
A real repair quote would carry a real garage's details and a real claimant's, which is not
something to publish in training material.
